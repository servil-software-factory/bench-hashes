About the hashes compared

BLAKE3 official is the blake3 crate, the official implementation. BLAKE3-Servil is the implementation developed here. SHA-256 is the fastest implementation measured on each machine (the sha2 crate, ring, or the operating system's CommonCrypto); the tooltip names which. sha1sum and md5sum, drawn in grey, compute SHA-1 and MD5: both are broken as cryptographic hashes (collisions are cheap to make), and they appear here only because many people still run them as checksums; a comparison with them is a comparison with the tool in hand, not with a safe alternative.
